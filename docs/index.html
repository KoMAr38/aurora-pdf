Questions

Which computers does it run on?
Windows 10 (version 1809 or later) and Windows 11, 64-bit. It needs Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, which Windows 11 has and Windows 10 gets with Microsoft Edge.
Do I need an internet connection?
No. Everything, text recognition included, runs offline. A link inside a PDF opens in your browser only when you click it.
Will other people see my comments and signatures?
Yes. They are standard PDF comments, form fields and signatures, so Adobe Acrobat Reader and other PDF readers show them.
What happens when the trial ends?
The app stops opening until you buy it in the Microsoft Store. Your files stay where they are and open in any PDF reader.
Is redaction really permanent?
Yes. When you apply it, the text, picture pixels, comments, form fields and links under the mark are removed from the saved file. Keep a copy of the original if you need it.
Which languages?
The app is in English, Norwegian and Serbian. Text recognition reads English, Norwegian and Serbian in Latin and Cyrillic script.
